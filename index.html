How it works

Animate. Drag any joint — bones keep their length, so limbs swing instead of stretching. Drag the chest to move a whole figure. Use Flip to mirror a pose, Copy/Paste to carry one across frames.

Frames. The bottom tab shows thumbnails. + copies the current pose into a new frame, tap a thumbnail to switch, long-press for duplicate / move / delete.

Draw. Freehand strokes on the current frame — props, effects, whatever the figure holds.

Onion skin shows the previous frame faintly, which is how motion stays smooth.

Export gives an animated GIF, a PNG of the current frame, a video, or a project file. Everything stays on your device.
